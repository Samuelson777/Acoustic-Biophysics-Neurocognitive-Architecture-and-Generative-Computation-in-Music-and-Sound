Section 4

Neurocognitive Architecture of Syntax & Reward

Section Context: Reconciling domain-specific representations with domain-general prefrontal control under Patel's Shared Syntactic Integration Resource Hypothesis (SSIRH). Musical and linguistic structural violations both recruit BA 47, eliciting an identical P600 ERP component. In parallel, musical pleasure exhibits an anatomical temporal dissociation between anticipatory dorsal striatal dopamine and consummatory ventral striatal reward.

Neurochemical Striatal Dissociation

Molecular PET [^{11}{C}]raclopride displacement and fMRI BOLD data reveal two distinct striatal epochs during peak musical emotion:

1. Anticipatory Phase Right Caudate

Occurs during the 15s buildup preceding peak emotion (t = 3.2). Driven by structural expectation and tension.

2. Consummatory Phase Nucleus Accumbens

Occurs precisely during the peak chill / euphoria (t = 2.8, P < 0.001). Coupled to reward prediction error resolution.

Section 5

Clinical Neuromusicology & NMT Interventions

Section Context: Neurologic Music Therapy (NMT) uses rhythmic auditory entrainment to bypass basal ganglia pathology in movement and speech disorders. External periodic cues project through reticular and cerebellar pathways to entrain spinal motor neurons directly.

Sensorimotor

Rhythmic Auditory Stimulation (RAS)

Delivers periodic auditory pulses matched to gait cadence. Bypasses impaired nigrostriatal loops in Parkinson's disease, improving stride length and eliminating freezing of gait (FOG).

Speech & Language

Rhythmic Reading Training (RRT)

Synchronizes meter and linguistic syntax via shared prefrontal temporal coherence (BA 47), driving phonological decoding recovery in acquired post-stroke dyslexia.

Cognitive & Autonomic

Brainwave Entrainment (BWE)

Establishes frequency-following cortical synchronization to reduce migraine severity, regulate autonomic stress markers, and enhance inter-hemispheric brain coherence.

Section 6

Neural Audio Codecs & Generative AI

Section Context: Modern neural audio codecs compress continuous time-domain waveforms into discrete token streams. This section compares Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ), Finite Scalar Quantization (FSQ), Single Vector Quantization (SVQ), and Group Vector Quantization (GVQ) across bitrate efficiency, acoustic fidelity, language model context simplicity, and transmission noise robustness.

Vector Quantization (VQ) Trade-off Analysis

Different discrete quantization paradigms optimize specific points along the architectural efficiency frontier:

    RVQ (SoundStream/EnCodec/DAC): Cascades M hierarchical residual codebooks. Exceptional waveform fidelity; requires codebook delay interleaving patterns in downstream language models like MusicGen.
    FSQ (SQ-Codec/Spectral): Projects continuous latents into fixed discrete scalar values. Exceptional channel noise perturbation robustness without catastrophic residual misalignment.
    SVQ (BigCodec/WavTokenizer): Uses a single unified codebook (M=1), drastically simplifying language modeling to a single token stream.
